Overview

Introduction

This artifact examines model bias and fairness within a classification system trained on real-world data. It expands my portfolio beyond model development and data preparation by demonstrating ethical evaluation and responsible AI thinking.

The project emphasizes that a model should not be evaluated only by its overall performance. Practitioners must also determine whether the model behaves consistently across different groups and whether its predictions could create unfair outcomes.

Professional Value

Why This Artifact Matters

Ethical AI Evaluation

The artifact demonstrates my ability to think beyond accuracy and examine whether a classification model produces fair and valid results across different subgroups.

Real-World Relevance

Fairness audits are especially important when models influence sensitive decisions involving lending, employment, healthcare, or access to important services.

Audience Awareness

The project was refined so recruiters can quickly understand its importance while technical reviewers can examine the evaluation approach in greater detail.

Professional Communication

The language and presentation were adjusted to be more business-focused, readable, and appropriate for a professional portfolio.

Approach

Evaluation Process

1

Examine the Classification Model

Review the model's predictions and consider whether overall performance accurately represents its behavior across different populations.

2

Evaluate Group Differences

Compare results across subgroups to identify disparities that may not appear in a single overall performance score.

3

Interpret Fairness Findings

Consider whether observed differences could create unfair or harmful outcomes in practical decision-making settings.

4

Communicate Results Clearly

Present the most important findings in a format that is useful to hiring managers, business stakeholders, technical peers, and interviewers.

Reflection

What I Learned

This artifact taught me that strong academic work does not automatically become strong professional portfolio content. A professional artifact must be designed for its intended audience.

Recruiters often need to understand the value and impact quickly, while technical reviewers care more about accuracy, completeness, methods, and supporting evidence. I therefore had to balance simplicity with technical detail.

By adding ethical evaluation to my portfolio, this artifact strengthens the overall representation of my AI and machine learning abilities. It connects model development, data processing, communication, and responsible AI assessment.
